Theo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

Theo is a contraction timing app built by Nathan Chung. Your privacy matters — here is exactly what Theo does and does not do with your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

All contraction times, sessions, settings, and game scores are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to enable Partner Sharing.

No Analytics or Tracking

Theo does not collect analytics, usage data, or any form of tracking. There are no third-party analytics SDKs in the app.

Partner Sharing (Optional)

If you choose to enable Partner Sharing, you sign in with your email address via a one-time code. Theo then stores the following in Supabase (a cloud database) so your partner can see your contractions in real time:

  • Your email address
  • Your contraction timing data (start time, end time, duration, intensity)
  • Your partner connection (who you are paired with)

This data is only shared with the specific partner you invite. You can disconnect your partner or delete your synced data at any time from within the app.

Notifications

Theo may request permission to send local notifications to alert you when your contractions match a pattern. These notifications are generated entirely on your device — no data is sent to any server.

No Data Sold

Your data will never be sold to third parties. Period.

Data Deletion

You can delete all local data at any time from Settings → Clear All Data. If you have Partner Sharing enabled, you can disconnect and clear synced data from the Partner Sharing section in Settings.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Used only for Partner Sharing (authentication and data sync). Not used unless you opt in. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS — Used to build and distribute the app. Expo Privacy Policy
