Theo Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Summary: Theo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data stays on your device.

About Theo

Theo is a contraction timer app designed to help monitor labor contractions. The app provides timing tools, pattern alerts, and relaxation features to support you during labor.

Data Collection

Theo does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • No advertising networks or SDKs are integrated.
  • No account creation or sign-in is required.

Data Storage

All data you create in Theo, including contraction records, session history, and app settings, is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Notifications

Theo may request permission to send local notifications to alert you when contraction patterns meet certain thresholds (such as the 5-1-1 rule). These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers.

Third-Party Services

Theo does not use any third-party services that collect user data. The app functions entirely offline.

Children's Privacy

Theo is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
