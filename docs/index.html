Theo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

About Theo

Theo is a contraction timing app built by Nathan Chung. You can use its timing, history, and relaxation features without creating an account.

Data Stored on Your Device

Contraction records, session history, settings, and game scores are stored locally on your device. While Partner Sharing is enabled and you are connected to a partner, contraction records are also sent to Supabase.

Partner Sharing (Optional)

Partner Sharing uses your email address and a one-time code to sign you in. Supabase stores your email address, contraction timing data (start time, end time, duration, and intensity), partner invitation details (including the invited email address), and partner connection. This information supports authentication and sharing contraction records with your connected partner.

Sharing Controls and Data Deletion

You can disconnect a partner from Settings → Partner Sharing. Signing out or disconnecting does not delete your account or previously synced records. While signed in, use Settings → Account → Delete Account to request deletion of your account, synced contractions, and partnership data. If you are signed out, use Settings → Account → Delete an existing account to verify your email and access deletion. This sign-in is available while Partner Sharing is disabled and does not create an account or enable sharing. Account deletion does not remove the records stored locally on your device. Use Settings → Clear All Data to remove local records, settings, and game scores; this does not delete your cloud account or previously synced records.

Exports and Care Team Sharing

You can create a summary image or CSV of your local contraction records and share it with apps or people you choose using your device’s share sheet. Theo prepares these files on your device and does not upload them to our servers. Temporary export copies may remain in the app’s cache. Settings → Clear All Data removes cached exports and other local records. Copies you have already shared are controlled by the recipient or the other app.

Notifications

Theo may request permission to send local notifications when contraction patterns match your settings. These notifications are generated on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in your device settings.

No Analytics, Advertising, or Data Sales

Theo does not use advertising SDKs, track your activity for advertising, or sell your data. Optional support purchases require processing transaction information as described below.

Third-Party Services

Supabase provides account authentication and cloud storage for optional Partner Sharing. If support purchases are available, Apple or Google processes the payment and RevenueCat processes purchase records, a purchase identifier, and your Theo account identifier (or an anonymous identifier) to recognize and restore your support purchase. No contraction records are sent to RevenueCat, and Theo does not receive your payment card details. Payments are optional and do not restrict any feature. Expo / EAS builds and distributes the app. Provider policies: https://supabase.com/privacy, https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy, and https://expo.dev/privacy.

Feedback

Settings includes an optional feedback form that opens a draft in your email app. The draft includes your message and Theo’s version/build number; contraction records and exports are not attached. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email. If you send feedback, we receive the message and your email address to respond. Please avoid including private health information.
