Theo

A little calm, one contraction at a time.

A gentle contraction timer for iPhone, made by Nathan Chung. Track each contraction, look back over your sessions, and take a quiet moment with simple relaxation activities.

Free to use. Yours to keep.

The timer, history, and relaxation activities work without an account. Your records stay on your device. You can share a summary image or CSV with your midwife or doctor when you choose.

Optional Apple, Google, and email sign-in are for your Theo account. Signing in does not turn on partner sharing or upload your contraction history.

Theo helps you record timing patterns. It does not provide medical advice or replace your care team's guidance.
